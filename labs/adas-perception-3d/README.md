## Engineering Architecture

- **Zero-Build Modular ES6:** Clean Separation of Concerns across dedicated physics engines (`physics.js` / `engine.js`), procedural PBR texture generators (`textures.js`), WebGL renderers (`scene.js`), and minimalist HUD controllers (`main.js` / `ui.js`).
- **Real-Time WebGL & Three.js PBR:** Studio IBL reflections (`RoomEnvironment`), 2K soft shadows, ACES Filmic tone mapping, selective HDR Bloom, and GPU-instanced micro-geometry running smoothly at 60 FPS directly in the browser.
- **First-Principles Physics:** Every visual state is driven by analytical models — Roofline arithmetic intensity, closed-form Hessian eigendecomposition, Dijkstra shortest-path routing, closed-loop DVFS thermal throttling, and the LiDAR optical range equation.
