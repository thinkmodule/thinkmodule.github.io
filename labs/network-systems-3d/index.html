06 • Core Network Engineering Equations First-Principles Queueing, Routing & Transport Physics

Bandwidth-Delay Product (BDP)

BDP = BW × RTTmin ,   NMSS = BDP / 1460 B

The exact amount of unacknowledged data that must be in flight simultaneously to keep a long-distance fiber link 100% saturated without queuing bloat.

TCP CUBIC & Mathis Loss Bound

W(t) = C(t − K)³ + Wmax ,   Rate ∝ MSS / (RTT·√p)

Loss-based TCP algorithms slash CWND on packet drop, causing throughput to collapse inversely with √p on high-RTT links — motivating model-based BBR pacing.

Dijkstra Shortest-Path Routing

d[v] = min(d[v], d[u] + cost(u, v))  ∀ (u,v) ∈ Eup

Link-state protocols like OSPF flood link-down LSAs when a subsea fiber breaks, allowing every router to converge on the lowest-latency surviving path in milliseconds.
