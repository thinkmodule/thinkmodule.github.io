How It Works Under the Hood

Every step above runs the exact mathematical pipeline used inside modern transformer decoding loops:

1. Logits & Temperature

The neural network outputs raw unnormalized scores z. Dividing by Temperature z / T stretches or compresses differences before exponentiation.

2. Softmax Normalization

Applying exp(z/T) / sum(exp(z/T)) converts scaled scores into a valid probability distribution summing to 100%.

3. Top-K & Nucleus (Top-P)

Low-probability tail tokens are truncated to prevent hallucinations, and the remaining active pool is re-normalized back to 100%.

4. Shannon Entropy

Measured in bits via -Σ p log2(p). Near 0 bits means near-deterministic output; higher bits indicate greater branching uncertainty.
